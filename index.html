How it works

Four steps from booking to follow-up

Each stage has its own checklist, so you can see what to complete before your procedure and what to do afterwards.

  1. 01

    Book a consultation

    Choose a department or specialist and select an available time.

  2. 02

    Complete your paperwork

    Submit pre-admission forms, review your cost estimate and confirm your medical aid details.

  3. 03

    Check in and receive care

    Confirm your admission details and arrive for your procedure with everything on file.

  4. 04

    Track your recovery

    View discharge instructions and manage your follow-up appointments.
