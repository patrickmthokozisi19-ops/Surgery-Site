About Pweb

One platform. Many institutions.

Pweb is a platform that healthcare institutions use. It is not a hospital and does not provide treatment. Each institution gets its own branded experience, content and settings, built on the same shared foundation.

Pweb is being built step by step. This page describes where the platform is heading, not a finished list of features.

Designed for healthcare institutions of different sizes

From a single specialist practice to a group with several locations.

  • Private hospitals

    A coherent public presence for departments, facilities, specialists and patient enquiries.

  • Surgical practices

    A focused website that explains services and makes it easy to ask for a consultation.

  • Specialist medical practices

    A professional, easy-to-maintain presence for smaller teams without a dedicated web department.

  • Multi-location healthcare groups

    Shared standards across locations, with each site keeping its own details and branding.

Some capabilities described on this page are intended for later releases. Availability will grow as the platform is developed.
